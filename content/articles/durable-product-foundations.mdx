## Why Foundations Matter

Most product teams move faster when common systems are already settled. Authentication, account management, authorization, email workflows, API structure, and database access are not glamorous, but they shape every feature that follows.

## What Usually Pays Off

A durable foundation gives teams a few things early: a consistent data model, predictable route boundaries, clear user roles, and operational tools for support. Those choices reduce ambiguity when the product starts changing quickly.

## Where To Start

Start with the paths every product eventually needs: sign up, sign in, account recovery, profile management, admin visibility, and safe database migrations. Then add content and documentation around the decisions users and operators need to understand.
